COLLEGE ALGEBRA

College Algebra is a foundational mathematics course that explores the principles of algebraic reasoning, problem solving, and mathematical modeling. Topics typically include equations and inequalities, functions and their graphs, polynomial and rational expressions, exponential and logarithmic functions, and systems of equations. Mastery of college algebra provides essential skills for advanced study in mathematics, science, engineering, and related fields.

Key Concepts

Key concepts in College Algebra include foundational topics that are essential for understanding higher-level mathematics:

  • Equations and Inequalities: Techniques for solving linear, quadratic, polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic equations and inequalities.
  • Functions and Graphs: Understanding the definition of a function, domain and range, and how to graph linear, quadratic, polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions.
  • Polynomial and Rational Expressions: Simplifying, factoring, and performing operations on polynomials and rational expressions.
  • Systems of Equations: Methods for solving systems of linear and nonlinear equations, including substitution, elimination, and matrix approaches.
  • Applications and Modeling: Using algebraic concepts to model and solve real-world problems.
